MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This MSA is entered into between Cobalt Financial Partners ("Client") and
Westbrook Consulting Group ("Consultant") effective May 11, 2026.

Consultant will deliver an enterprise data governance framework. Total fees
under the initial work order are $220,000 USD. The parties expect the
initial work order to run roughly six weeks, subject to data access.

Deliverables: governance operating model; data quality scorecard; policy
library.

Invoices payable net 30.
