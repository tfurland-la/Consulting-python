How this works

Every CCA-F task statement carries a weight. Miss a question and its statement's weight rises (×1.5, capped at 5.0); get it right and the weight falls (×0.7, floored at 0.5). The next question is drawn at random, biased by those weights and by the real exam's domain mix — so practice naturally concentrates on your weak spots and backs off as you master them.

The five most recently tested statements sit out each draw, so short runs stay varied. Progress persists between sessions; the weak-spots list shows where the tool currently thinks you should work.

In the desktop app, fresh questions are generated on demand through your local Claude Code. Opened as a plain page, questions come from the reviewed bank in questions.js instead.
